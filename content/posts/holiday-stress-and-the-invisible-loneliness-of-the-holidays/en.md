The festive season is supposed to be a time of joy and togetherness. For many people, especially those living far from family, it can also bring pressure, tiredness and a loneliness nobody notices.

In this column for BG BEN’s “School of Psychology”, I write about holiday stress and the invisible loneliness of the holidays, and how we can look after ourselves and each other at this time of year.
