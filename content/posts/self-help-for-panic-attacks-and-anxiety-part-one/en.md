A panic attack can be frightening: a racing heart, shortness of breath and a sudden feeling that something terrible is about to happen. Understanding what is going on in the body is the first step towards feeling calmer.

In this column for BG BEN’s “School of Psychology”, I write about panic attacks and anxiety, and the self-help steps you can start with. The series continues in part two.
