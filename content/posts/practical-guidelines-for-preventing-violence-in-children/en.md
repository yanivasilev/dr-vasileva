Violence among children rarely appears out of nowhere. It often grows from difficult feelings, from examples children see around them and from problems that adults miss.

In this column for BG BEN’s “School of Psychology”, I write about practical guidelines that parents, teachers and carers can use to prevent violence, and to respond when it happens.
