The experiences, needs and feelings of our childhood stay with us long after we grow up. They shape how we react, how we love and how we look after ourselves as adults.

In this column for BG BEN’s “School of Psychology”, I write about the child within us, and how understanding that inner child can be a path back to ourselves.
