Emotional intelligence is our ability to recognise, understand and manage our own feelings and the feelings of others. It shapes our relationships, our work and our wellbeing.

In this column for BG BEN’s “School of Psychology”, I write about what emotional intelligence really means, and how to see where we are with it.
