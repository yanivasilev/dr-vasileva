title: Invitation to happiness
slug: invitation-to-happiness
summary: Happiness is not only something that happens to us. A column about inviting it into our lives.
---
We often think of happiness as something that comes from outside: the right job, the right moment, the right person. Yet a great deal of it depends on how we live and look at each day.

In this column for BG BEN’s “School of Psychology”, I write about happiness as an invitation we can accept, and the small choices that bring it closer.
