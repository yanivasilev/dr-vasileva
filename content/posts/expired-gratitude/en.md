Gratitude is one of the quiet strengths of a good relationship, but it is easy to take kindness for granted once it becomes familiar.

In this column for BG BEN’s “School of Psychology”, I write about what happens when gratitude seems to have an expiry date, and why it is worth keeping it alive.
