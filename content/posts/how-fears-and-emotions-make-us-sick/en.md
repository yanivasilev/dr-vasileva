Mind and body are closely connected. Long-lasting fear, worry and unexpressed emotions do not stay only in our thoughts; they can affect our sleep, our energy and our physical health.

In this column for BG BEN’s “School of Psychology”, I write about how fears and emotions can make us ill, and why caring for our emotional health means caring for our whole health.
