Holding on to hurt and resentment can keep us tied to the past. Forgiveness does not mean forgetting or excusing what happened; it can be a way of setting ourselves free.

In this column for BG BEN’s “School of Psychology”, I write about forgiveness as one of life’s greatest teachers, and what it can teach us.
