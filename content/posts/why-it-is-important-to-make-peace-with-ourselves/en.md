Many of us are far harsher with ourselves than we would ever be with a friend. Constant self-criticism and inner conflict can quietly drain our energy and our joy.

In this column for BG BEN’s “School of Psychology”, I write about why making peace with ourselves matters, for our own wellbeing and for our relationships with others.
