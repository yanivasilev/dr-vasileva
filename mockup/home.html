About me

Hello, I'm Mirena

For 21 years, since 2005, I have been doing the work I love most: helping people understand themselves, heal and find their way forward.

  • 01

    PhD in Developmental Psychology

    My research explored how the family shapes the way teenagers cope with stress.

  • 02

    Practical, evidence-based CBT

    Tools that work in your everyday life, at a pace that feels right for you.

  • 03

    Between two countries

    Born in Bulgaria, in London since 2016. I know what it's like to build a life in two places.
